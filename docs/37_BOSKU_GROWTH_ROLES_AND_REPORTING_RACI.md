# BOSKU GROWTH ROLES & REPORTING RACI

Owner: pricing, promotions, commissions, stock, policy, business decisions, final commercial approval.

Growth Operator / Capster: daily observation, customer/source capture, SOP, eligible WhatsApp follow-up, content, daily report, weekly report, experiment execution.

Other Capster: service, transaction accuracy, customer observation, loyalty/source capture, issue reporting.

System Builder: documentation, data model, dashboard/automation after validation, implementation, testing, auditability.

Cadence: Daily Growth Report by Growth Operator; Weekly Growth Report by Growth Operator and reviewed by Owner; Monthly review by Owner + Growth Operator.
